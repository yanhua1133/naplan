- The story may be amusing or serious.
- Planning guidance explicitly covers setting, characters, complication, ending, paragraphs, vocabulary, spelling and punctuation.
- New prompts may change the toy and setting but must retain the `ordinary toy comes to life` narrative mechanism and comparable response burden.
